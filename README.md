## Featured Project

### [Exploring Startup Trends with SQL](https://github.com/fawariclement-lab/startup-trends-sql-analysis)

10 SQL queries analyzing startup funding, closures, acquisitions, and fund activity in a venture-capital dataset — with charts and documented findings.

![Average investment rounds by fund activity](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fawariclement-lab/startup-trends-sql-analysis/main/charts/average_investment_rounds_by_activity.svg)

- **2,584** companies in the dataset were marked closed
- US companies accounted for roughly **$310.6B** in total funding — far ahead of any other country
- High-activity funds averaged **252** investment rounds, versus just **2** for low-activity funds
